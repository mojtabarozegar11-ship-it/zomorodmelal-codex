# Weekly Evolution Policy

## Objective
Worker Mojtaba must, once each week, evaluate itself and progress toward a new implementation stage through measurable engineering work.

## Weekly cycle
- Review the previous week's operation, failures, costs, security events, and completed tasks.
- Identify the highest-impact unfinished capabilities.
- Research and learn validated implementation approaches.
- Define one or more concrete upgrade objectives.
- Implement, test, and verify the changes.
- Create a versioned upgrade record and rollback point.
- Update the stage/status only when the new capability is actually implemented and tested.

## Stage progression
Stages are capability milestones, not calendar labels. A weekly cycle may:
- advance one stage,
- remain at the current stage while fixing blockers,
- or advance multiple stages when several independently verified milestones are completed.

## Safety and truthfulness
- Do not mark an upgrade complete without evidence.
- Do not claim access to a service, account, device, market, bank, or provider without an actual authorized connection.
- Financial actions remain restricted to real authorization, configured limits, and applicable legal/provider requirements.
- Keep credentials, private keys, seed phrases, passwords, and signing secrets out of source code and ordinary memory.
- Every self-upgrade must be auditable and reversible where technically feasible.

## Owner reporting
Each weekly cycle must produce a concise record containing:
- previous stage
- new stage or blocked status
- implemented capabilities
- tests/evidence
- remaining blockers
- next week's objectives
